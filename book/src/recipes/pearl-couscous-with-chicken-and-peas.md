# Pearl Couscous with Chicken and Peas

{{#include cook-mode.md}}

## Ingredients
* 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
* 1 \\( \frac{1}{3} \\) cups (about 7 ounces) pearl (Israeli) couscous
* 2 \\( \frac{1}{2} \\) cups low-sodium chicken broth
* Zest of 2 large lemons
* \\( \frac{1}{4} \\) cup fresh lemon juice (from 1 large lemon)
* 2 teaspoons kosher salt
* \\( \frac{1}{4} \\) teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
* \\( \frac{1}{2} \\) cup freshly grated Parmesan
* 4 green onions, chopped
* 1 cup frozen peas, thawed
* 1 rotisserie chicken breast, chopped to yield 1 cup of meat

## Instructions
* In a large saucepan, heat the oil over medium-high heat. Add the couscous and cook until lightly toasted, 2 to 3 minutes.
* Add the broth, lemon zest, lemon juice, salt and pepper. Bring to a boil.
* Reduce the heat to medium-low and simmer uncovered until the couscous is tender but still firm to the bite, about 8 minutes.
* Stir in the cheese, green onions, peas and chicken. Spoon into bowls and serve.
