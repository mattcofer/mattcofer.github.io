# Cheeseburger Soup

<https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/13062/cheeseburger-soup-i/>

{{#include cook-mode.md}}

Note: makes 8 servings

## Ingredients

### Soup Base
* 1 tablespoon butter
* \\( \frac{1}{2} \\) pound ground beef
* \\( \frac{3}{4} \\) cup chopped onion
* \\( \frac{3}{4} \\) cup shredded carrots
* \\( \frac{3}{4} \\) cup chopped celery
* 4 cups cubed potatoes
* 3 cups chicken broth
* 1 teaspoon dried basil
* 1 teaspoon dried parsley

### Cheese Sauce & Finish
* 3 tablespoons butter
* \\( \frac{1}{4} \\) cup all-purpose flour
* 1 \\( \frac{1}{2} \\) cups milk
* 2 cups cubed Cheddar cheese (or shredded)
* \\( \frac{1}{4} \\) cup sour cream
* Sliced green onions (for garnish)

## Instructions
* **Brown Beef and Veggies**: Melt 1 tablespoon butter in a large pot over medium heat. Add ground beef, onion, carrots, and celery. Cook and stir until beef is browned and crumbly, 5 to 7 minutes.
* **Simmer Potatoes**: Stir in potatoes, chicken broth, basil, and parsley; bring soup to a boil. Reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer until potatoes are tender, 10 to 12 minutes.
* **Make Roux & White Sauce**: In a separate small saucepan, melt remaining 3 tablespoons butter over medium heat. Add flour and whisk until smooth, about 1 minute. Gradually whisk in milk. Simmer, whisking constantly, until sauce is thick and smooth.
* **Combine and Melt Cheese**: Pour sauce into the soup mixture, stirring constantly. Bring to a boil over high heat; reduce heat to low. Add cheddar cheese and stir until completely melted.
* **Finish**: Stir in sour cream until just heated through.
* **Serve**: Serve hot, garnished with sliced green onions.
